The Fan
Is the Brief

A Creative Strategy Lens on 28 Music Festivals.

Pooja Pisharody
01 · The cultural moment
The festival is no longer the event.
It’s the artifact.

Fans build the experience before, during, and after: the trip, the playlists, the group, and the feed. The lineup is an invitation, but identity is the product that generates its own economic value. Each festival also speaks in a different cultural register: Lollapalooza is Diversity, Austin City Limits is Texas Roots, Governors Ball is New York, so the brand is a point of view, not a logo.

Pooja Pisharody
02 · The brief
Objective · Audience · Tension
ObjectiveGrow fan loyalty across global festivals by building belonging that compounds across festivals.
AudienceSpecific to the location and genre, targeted fan demographics that treat festivals as part of who they are.
TensionBuild one fan relationship without flattening what makes each festival feel different.

04 · Visual system
Shared tone,
local accent.

Poster logic: one idea, one color, and one typographic voice per festival. One underlying design system so 28 global festivals resonate as a family. Designed to work at scale across mediums: feed, wristbands, merch.

05 · My Concept
One Fan ID.
Every Festival.

Fan Passport: a cross-festival identity that carries a fan’s history, badges, and data from one festival to the next. Shifting to measure how identity gets earned over time instead of assigned.

The Shelf
Is the Interface

A creative strategy lens on offline retail and next-generation devices.

Pooja Pisharody
01 · The problem
In the offline channel, the shelf is the first and final impression.

In-store, a device competes with dozens of others at the same eye level. Brand scale dilutes to packaging, fixture and placement, so despite the size of the company, attention still has to be earned one shelf at a time.

Pooja Pisharody
02 · The brief
Convert glance to trust
ObjectiveMove a shopper from glance to understanding to trust at the point of purchase.
AudienceConsidered-purchase shoppers making comparison judgments in minutes, across retailers with research-led and convenience-led intent.
TensionCommunicate an unfamiliar capability with credibility at a glance, while holding one brand system across environments we don't control.
Pooja Pisharody
03 · Strategy lens
New Tech × Gen AI

The product has to be legible before it's desirable.

Story over specsLead with what it does for someone.
Demo over descriptionLet the product explain itself.
One system, many retailersA shared grammar that flexes to each environment.

04 · Visual system
One focus per fixture

One focal product per display, inside a shared color and type system.

Then measure it. Track ROI by fixture and touchpoint analytics (engagement, dwell, conversion) to iterate on what earns the focus.
